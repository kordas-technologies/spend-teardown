## 1. If we emailed you about a teardown

- **What we hold:** your name (if it was published), your business email address, your
  company name and the public source that led us to you (for example a public repository
  that contains Claude Code configuration, or a public post by your company).
- **Where we got it:** from your company's public website (contact page, footer,
  security.txt) or a public company profile. We do not take email addresses from git
  commit metadata.
- **Why and on what basis:** to offer a free teardown to a business, based on our
  legitimate interest in business-to-business outreach (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **How long:** if you don't reply, we delete your data 90 days after our last email. If you
  reply "stop", we keep only your email address on a suppression list so that we never
  contact you again.
- **Your rights:** you can object at any time (Art. 21), and ask for access, rectification,
  erasure or restriction. Reply "stop" or write to biuro@kordas.tech. You can also complain
  to the Polish supervisory authority (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl)
  or to the authority where you live or work.

## 2. If you send us an export

- The export contains aggregated token counts and costs per day and model, salted project
  hashes and mechanism counters. It is built to contain no paths, usernames, session ids,
  message contents or times of day (see SECURITY.md).
- **Why and on what basis:** to write your teardown report (Art. 6(1)(b), steps you asked
  for) and, only if you opt in, to include anonymised numbers in a case study or an
  aggregate benchmark with at least 5 teams per figure (Art. 6(1)(a), consent you can
  withdraw at any time).
- **How long:** 30 days after we send your report, unless you opt in to the benchmark.
- **Processors:** we use AI tools (Anthropic) to help draft reports from the aggregated
  export; a human reviews every report. Email is hosted by Google.
